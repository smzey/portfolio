## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a personal passbook — a private ledger that never leaves its owner, like AI that never leaves the device. Refuses the dark-dev-portfolio default (zinc tokens, glow cards, 3D hero, metric tiles).

OWN-WORLD: Navy passbook cover around mint guilloche security paper; carbon-blue ink; one vermilion teller-stamp red for verified states and misses. Anuphan for all prose (Thai + Latin), dot-matrix (Doto) only for printed ledger figures. Ruled ledger lines, no cards, no shadows or glows; depth only by page overlap.

STORY: The visitor reads who holds the account and the one claim, sees projects as printed ledger entries with their proof figure and cloud-call count, prints a live SignBridge prediction themselves, opens a statement to verify, then reaches the back cover to email or download the CV.

FIRST VIEWPORT: An open passbook spread on the navy cover. Left page: kicker "Account holder", name at display scale, Thai name, thesis "AI that runs on your device and never leaves it.", account details (type, branch, availability, GPA), Download CV primary + Copy email. Right page: ledger header, project entries, a print strip of 8 sample hands under it with the honesty note; the newest printed line sits at the top of the ledger. Primary action: Download CV (left page); signature action: tap a hand.

FORM: Thai bank passbook (สมุดบัญชี), grounded list position 5 of 7, seed:1049ef1a9de5b8a8. Raises: every figure prints its condition (oscilloscope); every entry carries provenance (quilt); no shadows, depth by overlap (papercut); each case study is its own statement page (matchbook); verified states take a teller stamp (coffeehouse).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
